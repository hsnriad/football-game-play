FOOTBALLCARD DUEL

Drop players. Win the ball. Score.

How to play
  1. Drag a card onto the pitch. The closest player runs to the ball and carries it straight at goal.
  2. Two players per side. A third card replaces your player who doesn't have the ball.
  3. Pass: when you have the ball, tap your other player. Passes can be caught on the way.
  4. Defend from the front. A player standing in front of the runner wins the ball. Players behind can't catch it.
  5. Shoot! Near goal the game pauses: tap where to shoot. When they shoot, tap where your keeper dives.
  6. Cards: Sprinter is fast but can't tackle. Defender tackles wide. Passes to the Playmaker can't be intercepted.
